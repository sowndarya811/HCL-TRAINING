Nature is the physical and natural world that surrounds us, 
including all living and non-living things. 
It provides essential elements for life, such as fresh air to breathe, clean water to drink, soil to grow food, and sunlight. 
From tall green trees and flowing rivers to animals and changing seasons, nature maintains a delicate ecological balance.
However, human activities like deforestation, pollution, and global warming threaten this balance.
Protecting nature through planting trees, saving water, and reducing waste is vital to ensure a healthy future for all living beings on Earth
